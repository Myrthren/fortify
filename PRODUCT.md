# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Fortify serves online business owners and small operating teams who need to decide what to do, turn that decision into a plan, and execute through connected business tools. The current product supports both first-time operators and experienced users who may skip guidance.

## Product Purpose

Fortify is an operating workspace for online businesses. It combines business context, decisions, plans, content, leads, connected services, automation and agent-based simulations so that work produces a saved, inspectable result instead of ending as an isolated AI conversation.

Success means a member can describe a business once, receive a relevant operating path, use the right Fortify tools, and understand what agents and automations are doing on that business's behalf.

## Positioning

Fortify turns persistent business context into plans, procedures and observable agent activity. Its simulation environments expose decisions, resources, constraints and collaboration as a world a user can inspect and change.

## Operating Context

Members work from a web dashboard with Company DNA, decision and content workspaces, future plans, procedural workflows, connected business software and a 3D Simulation Lab. Work is tied to subscription tiers and must remain usable on desktop and mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Guided business setup must ask only for information needed to produce a useful first operating path.
- Business limits are plan-based: Free has no saved businesses, Pro has 1, Elite has 5 and Apex is unlimited.
- Users may skip guided setup and explore the product directly.
- Business setup must create persistent context that other Fortify tools can use.
- The Simulation Lab must start with three concrete environment themes while preserving one deterministic, replayable simulation state.
- The 3D view must remain adaptive for older GPUs, respect reduced motion, suspend offscreen rendering and retain a non-WebGL fallback.
- No claim that a generated plan, simulation outcome or forecast is a verified business result.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Fortify. The visual language uses the graphite-and-silver palette, the original/classic and science-fiction themes, precise instrument-like controls, restrained radii and dotted signal motifs. Archivo is the primary brand typeface, with Inter Tight for supporting text and dense product controls. New work extends that identity.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing dashboard and navigation implementation under `web/app` and `web/components`.
- Persistent Company DNA, plans, workflows, connected tools and tier controls.
- A working deterministic Research Institute simulation with six agents, replay, interventions and adaptive Three.js rendering.
- Current simulation character and environment assets are local to the repository.
- No customer testimonials, performance claims or outcome benchmarks are supplied for this update.

## Product Principles

1. Reach a real saved result quickly.
2. Show what agents are doing and why.
3. Connect advice to plans, procedures and evidence.
4. Keep autonomy bounded by explicit permissions and limits.
5. Make complex systems explorable without making ordinary tasks harder.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Interactive work must remain keyboard reachable, maintain visible focus, respect reduced-motion preferences, expose text alternatives for the 3D view and remain operable when WebGL is unavailable.
