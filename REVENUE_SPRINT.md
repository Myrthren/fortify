# Fortify: first paid customers, 12–26 October 2026

## Goal and evidence

Use £300 in collected subscription payments as the working definition of “hundreds”. Four Elite subscriptions at £79 would be £316 gross; two Apex subscriptions at £199 would be £398; eleven Pro subscriptions at £29 would be £319. These are target combinations, not a forecast or profit: provider/payment costs, tax and refunds still matter. Count successful payments separately from recurring run rate and interested replies.

The strongest demonstrable offer in this release is: “Turn a competitor page into useful content drafts in your saved voice, then monitor meaningful changes.” Show actual source quotes, one relevant draft and the selected business/client context. Do not lead with cancelling five tools until a buyer identifies five existing jobs that Fortify demonstrably replaces.

## Focus

Prioritise existing researched rows for solo operators and small agency owners who create content repeatedly and already work in Discord. Select from K's supplied signals and personal hooks; do not research new leads or infer buying intent from a fit score. Client contexts support one owner's separated client work, not staff permissions. Do not sell team collaboration as shipped.

Elite is the relevant plan for the automated watch/workflow demonstration. Pro is suitable for drafting tools and private contexts; do not show an Elite workflow and imply Pro includes it. Keep the existing published prices. Offer founder-assisted setup as K's personal assistance with clear scope, not a new automatic product entitlement.

## Outreach operation

Continue K's existing sender limits and safeguards: up to 20 eligible first emails plus five eligible single follow-ups per weekday, at most 150 sends in rolling seven days. Fewer eligible leads means fewer sends. Respect the inbox restriction, suppressions, original country/company checks and supplied-row-only personalisation. K handles replies; the sender does not negotiate or reply for him. Mailbox verification inside Fortify is separate from the daily MCPEmails sender and does not replace its compliance checks.

For ten sending weekdays the maximum is 200 new contacts and 50 follow-ups. Four buyers from 200 new contacts would require 2% contact-to-paid conversion; there is no measured basis yet for assuming that rate. Eligibility, replies and founder follow-through may reduce reachable volume. Do not raise volume to compensate for an unclear offer.

## Measure and adjust

Record eligible contacts, scheduled/sent emails, bounces, opt-outs, interested replies, demos completed, first useful drafts, checkout starts, completed payments and cancellations, split by supplied segment. Use contacts sent as the denominator for reply conversion; use qualified demos as the denominator for demo-to-paid conversion. Do not rely on opens as proof of interest.

After the first 40–60 eligible sends, inspect actual replies and delivery failures. No interested replies calls for reviewing segment fit, observation and offer; interested replies without a demo calls for simplifying K's next step; useful demos without payment calls for inspecting price, trust, missing capability and checkout. Make one change at a time and retain the original version in the ledger notes.

Before broader outreach, K needs one real, authorised example he can walk through: source page -> observed facts -> branded draft -> saved run. Use screenshots or a short recording from that actual run, never fabricated customer results. Ask paying users which part they would use again, then fix that recurring task before expanding the feature list.
